## 1. Analytical Stance & Epistemic Boundaries

This memorandum explicitly avoids starting from a pre-determined thesis (such as declaring "collapse debunked" or "collapse proven"). Instead, it systematically interrogates eight competing structural hypotheses using empirical data collected across 2020–2026.

### Core Methodological Rules:
1. **Zero Premature Dogmatism**: A 2025 interaction rebound does NOT automatically disprove long-term secular stagnation or contraction.
2. **Consecutive Interval Analysis**: Structural shifts must be evaluated across discrete year-over-year boundaries (2022 $\to$ 2023, 2023 $\to$ 2024, 2024 $\to$ 2025).
3. **Quarantine of 2026 YTD**: 2026 represents a partial-year snapshot (Jan–Sep 2026). Total event/account volumes from 2026 YTD MUST NEVER be directly compared to 12-month calendar totals.
4. **Fail-Closed Commercial Valuation**: In the absence of private agency corporate balance sheets, talent payout contracts, and YouTube Studio back-end watch-time, macro financial metrics remain strictly `UNANSWERABLE_WITH_CURRENT_DATA`.
5. **Active Disconfirmation**: For every hypothesis, the strongest contradictory evidence must be explicitly evaluated and weighed.

## 3. Consecutive Period Comparative Trajectory Analysis

To avoid misinterpreting multi-year trends, structural shifts must be evaluated across discrete consecutive intervals:

### Period 1: 2022 $\to$ 2023 (Pioneer Influx to Supply Peak)
- **Active Channels:** Grew from 107 to 164 (+53.3%).
- **Audience Population:** Grew from 13,873 to 18,646 (+34.4%).
- **Newly Observed Accounts:** Peaked at 17,117 accounts.
- **Structural Driver:** High public curiosity, widespread debuts of agency generations (Pixela Gen 1 & 2, ARP Eclipse/Dusk/Dawn), proliferation of indie setups.
- **Vulnerability Introduced:** Supply grew faster than loyal retention; 91.8% of 2023 accounts were one-time or first-time observers.

### Period 2: 2023 $\to$ 2024 (Acute Contraction & Organizational Shakeout)
- **Active Channels:** Remained essentially flat (164 to 160, -2.4%).
- **Audience Population:** Dropped sharply from 18,646 to 13,478 (-27.7%).
- **Newly Observed Accounts:** Collapsed from 17,117 to 11,298 (-34.0%).
- **Structural Driver:** Post-pandemic screen-time normalization, graduation wave of 1st-gen agency talents, closure of unviable circles (RPG).
- **Network Reaction:** Modularity held stable (0.316 $\to$ 0.317), proving the underlying community topology did not fracture despite the volume loss.

### Period 3: 2024 $\to$ 2025 (Partial Rebound vs. Modularity Bifurcation)
- **Active Channels:** Rose slightly from 160 to 166 (+3.8%).
- **Audience Population:** Rebounded from 13,478 to 17,119 (+27.0%).
- **Weighted Edge Strength:** Surged to an all-time high of 7,029.
- **Structural Driver:** Consolidation around major agency events, cross-unit collabs, return of reactivated audiences (874 accounts, +5.1%), introduction of corporate entries like AStars (Brave Group APAC).
- **Critical Caveat:** While audience volume returned near 2023 levels, cross-agency edge share hit a historical low (45.6%), indicating an audience that returned into more isolated agency clusters.

### Period 4: 2026 YTD (Methodological Quarantine: Partial Year)
- **Active Channels Observed:** 140 (as of September 2026).
- **Audience Population Observed:** 9,218 accounts (partial 8-month sample).
- **Analytical Prohibition:** Comparing the 9,218 accounts directly to full 12-month totals (17,119 in 2025) is methodologically INVALID.
- **Valid Ratios Only:** Returning audience share in 2026 YTD reached a record 20.94% (1,930 / 9,218), confirming that long-term loyal audience persistence remains intact.

## 4. Rigorous Self-Falsification: Counter-Evidence to the Rebound Thesis

An analyst must actively challenge their own preferred interpretation. A 2025 population rebound (+27.0%) alone **does NOT disprove long-term structural contraction**. The following counter-arguments remain potent:

1. **Extreme Churn Rate (~91% Annual Attrition):**
   Even in 2025, 82.3% of interacting accounts were newly observed, and only 17.7% had been seen in prior years. If the inflow of new curious viewers dries up due to platform algorithmic shifts or platform saturation, the ecosystem lacks a wide enough loyal base to sustain current channel headcounts.

2. **Single-Channel Fragility (~90%–92% Single Channel Breadth):**
   Fewer than 10% of interacting viewers engage with more than one VTuber in a given year. The ecosystem is not a deeply interconnected common market, but rather a collection of mostly parallel, isolated fanbases sharing a small nomadic core.

3. **Key-Person Risk & Flagship Vulnerability:**
   Over 50% of the entire ecosystem's co-viewing edge weight depends on the top 10 bridging creators. If 3–5 flagship talents retire, significant portions of the inter-community graph could collapse.

4. **Addressable Market Ceiling:**
   Unlike English or Japanese VTuber ecosystems with global export potential, the Thai-language VTuber scene is bounded by Thailand's domestic internet demographics (~50–60 million online population). Influx numbers may be approaching a natural demographic ceiling.

## 5. Epistemic Synthesis & Concluding Evaluation

To the recurring community question: **“วงการวีไทยกำลังล่มสลายจริงหรือ?”**:

The empirical data rejects the simplistic dichotomy of either **“Unchecked Boom”** or **“Terminal Collapse”**.

The Thai VTuber ecosystem is best characterized as **Institutional Maturation (H2) and Niche Subcultural Stability (H5) under Severe Supply Saturation (H4)**:
- **It is NOT collapsing:** The network remains 95%–100% topologically connected; returning and reactivated audiences are expanding in percentage terms; corporate agencies continue to organize ticketed physical events; and total interaction connectivity hit all-time highs in 2025.
- **It is NOT effortlessly expanding:** Interacting audience volume is volatile and churns rapidly (~91% annual turnover); indie creators face fierce visibility constraints; and cross-agency interaction has clustered into agency silos.
- **Future Viability Depends On:** Deepening per-viewer retention and monetizable loyalty rather than pursuing indiscriminate creator recruitment.
