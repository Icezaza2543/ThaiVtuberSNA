## Temporal visibility rules

1. Period membership requires dated evidence of that virtual identity overlapping the selected window. Channel creation/upload age alone is insufficient. A confirmed virtual-identity start in 2023 excludes its pre-identity 2020 material; a creator discovered in 2026 is eligible in 2020 if reviewed identity-specific evidence supports 2020 activity. Discovery date is provenance, not an eligibility cutoff.
2. Use three outcomes: evidenced presence, evidenced outside the identity interval, and unknown/insufficient coverage. Unknown creators are not counted as confirmed absences; show a separate coverage/unknown count and explain why they are not asserted as period nodes. Do not infer continuous activity between distant observations or carry first-observed dates backward. Conflicting dates go to review.
3. Build the node set from qualifying identity/activity evidence, not from nonzero edges. Keep evidenced isolated nodes and nodes whose edges disappear at a threshold. Edges must belong to the selected window and connect eligible endpoints. Preserve canonical dated `interaction_time`; never substitute upload time for an undated interaction. Counts use the same period node set and current filters; distinguish period roster, visible roster and excluded/unknown coverage. Inspector/search cannot retain a node from the previous period.
4. Historical subscriber counts, agency, status, tier/size and centrality must come from dated observations or reviewed effective intervals. Otherwise show unknown, neutral size and no historical agency filter match. Current metadata may appear only in a separately labeled current-context section; never drive historical counts, sizing or rank. Manual island coordinates remain byte-identical and are layout anchors, not affiliation evidence; historical unknowns use a neutral placement rule without changing those constants.
5. Yearly = evidence within that year; cumulative = union of qualifying dated evidence up to the selected cutoff, explicitly labeled as cumulative rather than currently active. All-Time = available dated evidence only. Labels and metrics must state the same mode.
6. “Present” points to the latest collected snapshot and displays its actual cutoff, not the wall clock or build timestamp. Construct slider steps from available snapshot metadata. A future/missing period shows NO_SNAPSHOT (not fabricated zero activity or carried-forward nodes); add new periods only after real evidence passes validation. Undated legacy fallback is labeled separately and cannot masquerade as a historical slice.

## Backfill, budgets and resume

**Universe:** every approved YouTube channel in a new versioned manifest, independent of subscribers/tier/agency. Discovery candidates, directory counts and the frozen 193-channel cohort are not interchangeable. Use a fair round-robin first pass across all approved channels; tier may stratify pilot evaluation but cannot exclude low-tier channels from eventual coverage.

**First pass:** up to 1,000 distinct accessible videos per channel, or fewer if exhausted. Deleted/private placeholders and duplicate playlist entries do not consume the accessible-video allowance; they consume requests and are counted separately. Use 2020 as a coverage diagnostic, not the default catalog stop: disable the legacy early-date cutoff for this first pass so it continues until 1,000 accessible videos or exhaustion (unless explicitly paused by budget/error). Any separately approved cutoff policy must retain its own stop reason; cutoff, cap, exhaustion and budget stop are different states. Hitting 1,000 may stop in 2024 and never proves coverage to 2020. Do not assume a newest-first API page ordering is sufficient proof of a cutoff; validate ordering and continue/review anomalies. Exhaustion describes accessible catalog at collection time, not deleted/private history. Deeper-than-cap work needs a separate budget approval.

Keep title metadata from playlist snippets with the canonical video ID. Fetch additional video metadata in batches only when necessary; record which endpoint supported each date/title, and never merge the auxiliary title catalog into the historical catalog by title alone. Titles/descriptions used for attribution are discovery evidence, not automatic collaboration verification. Do not persist full descriptions indiscriminately.

**Proposed pilot, not yet authorized:** 12 explicitly approved channels spanning available size tiers and boundary cases (pre-existing channel/new identity, late discovery/old activity, low-volume isolated channel, cap-hit channel). Catalog ceiling 12,000 accessible videos. Then 4 dated videos per channel (48 total), stratified across accessible years/volume; absence of old videos is reported, not filled by substitutes with invented dates. Interaction pilot caps at 500 combined processed top-level-comment plus reply records per video, 24,000 total; replies consume the same cap, not an extra allowance; cap-hit jobs remain PARTIAL. This pilot tests operability, not representative audience estimates or completed history.

Measure serialized cell sizes and reject oversized payloads before writing rather than truncating them.
